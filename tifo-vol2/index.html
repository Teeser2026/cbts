A Call to Arms

The club is dictating the atmosphere at Selhurst with its own production plan: music drowning out our songs, flashing lights and fireworks.

This album is our answer. A message to the club to change direction, and to listen to the people who make the noise.

Between us, let’s fill in the empty tracks. Tell me a Palace moment that’s yours. Something that lives with you. Something no light show could ever make.

Then tell me the song that sums it up.

They’ll know us by our noise!
